# 09 — Merging

## Outcomes

- merge independent work;
- understand fast-forward and merge commits;
- inspect resulting history;
- reason about integration.

## Guided lab

Perform one fast-forward merge and one divergent-history merge in disposable repositories. Inspect both graphs.

## Practice drills

- merge a simple feature;
- merge divergent branches;
- inspect resulting history;
- verify files after merging.

## Break-It lab

Create a merge where both branches modify different files. Merge and inspect the result.

## Challenge

A colleague claims every merge creates a new commit. Prove or disprove with two lab histories and explain the result.

## Assignment

Create two independent work streams and integrate them. Submit the graph and a short explanation of why the final history looks as it does.

## Check guidance

Check both final content and history shape. Accept valid workflow variations when the learner explains them.
