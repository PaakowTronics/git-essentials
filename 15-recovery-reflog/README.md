# 15 — Recovery & Reflog

## Outcomes

- understand reachable history versus reference movement;
- inspect reflog;
- recover a seemingly lost local commit.

## Guided lab

Create several commits, record a known commit, perform a controlled history movement in a disposable repository, inspect normal history, then inspect reflog and recover the commit.

## Practice drills

- locate a previous HEAD position;
- identify a recovery target;
- inspect the recovered commit;
- restore the desired state.

## Challenge

Someone says, "The commit is gone." Your first task is to determine whether that is actually true.

## Assignment

Complete a recovery scenario and write an incident report showing evidence before and after recovery.

## Check guidance

The learner should stop making random changes and investigate references/history first.
